How liquid glass stays fast on budget phones

Glassmorphism is GPU-hungry, and much of the Indian market runs budget Android. The design keeps the silky swipe by spending blur only where it pays:

  • Glass on the card and dock only — two blur surfaces per screen, not twenty.
  • The aurora background is static — pre-rendered gradients per category (Geopolitics = ember red-blue, IPO = green-gold…), no live blur behind the feed.
  • Low-end fallback — devices that can't sustain 60fps get semi-transparent solid cards; same layout, same beauty at arm's length.
  • Category-tinted auroras — the background hue itself communicates the news type before a single word is read.

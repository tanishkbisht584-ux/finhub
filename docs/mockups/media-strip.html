FinSwipe — media strip
FinSwipe · feed card

Media strip, 16:9, between headline and summary

Rendered with the real tokens from app/lib/theme.dart. Photo and video are the same widget — video only adds a play badge and a tap target.

Placement

Between headline and summary, full card width, fixed 16:9. Everything below stays inside the existing scroll region, so nothing gets pushed off-screen.

Failure is silent

errorBuilder returns SizedBox.shrink(). A dead or blocked image URL collapses the strip rather than showing a broken-image icon.

One widget, two cases

A YouTube thumbnail is just an image URL. Video adds a play badge and an InkWell — no player, no webview, no new dependency.
